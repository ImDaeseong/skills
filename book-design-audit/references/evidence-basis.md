# Evidence basis and limits

Use these sources for bounded rationales, not as a universal house style.

## Standards and guidance

- W3C, *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2*: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
  - Supports minimum text contrast, text resizing/reflow, avoiding images of text, and robust text-spacing overrides.
  - WCAG 1.4.8's visual-presentation values are AAA customization criteria, not mandatory fixed typography values for every printed book.
- W3C, *EPUB Accessibility 1.1*: https://www.w3.org/TR/epub-a11y-11/
- W3C, *EPUB Accessibility Techniques 1.1*: https://www.w3.org/TR/epub-a11y-tech-11/
  - Supports accessible EPUB structure, alternatives, navigation, and the preference for actual text over images of text.

## Readability research

- Dyson, M. C., & Haselgrove, M. (2001), “The influence of reading speed and line length on the effectiveness of reading from screen,” *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies*, 54(4), 585–612. https://doi.org/10.1006/ijhc.2001.0458
- Shaikh, A. D., & Chaparro, B. S. (2005), “The Effects of Line Length on Reading Performance of Online News Articles.” https://doi.org/10.1177/154193120504900514
- Rello, L., Pielot, M., & Marcos, M.-C. (2016), “Make It Big! The Effect of Font Size and Line Spacing on Online Readability.” https://doi.org/10.1145/2858036.2858204

These studies support treating line length, type size, and spacing as interacting readability variables. They do not establish one optimum for Korean print books: their populations, scripts, devices, and tasks differ from that context. Use them to identify risks, then verify the actual rendered artifact and retain human judgment.

## Evidence labels

- **Supported:** the source directly supports the bounded statement.
- **Partial:** relevant but differs in script, medium, audience, or task.
- **Unverified:** no inspected primary source supports the claim.

Recheck URLs and current standards when a consequential claim depends on them. Record access limits rather than citing a search snippet as evidence.
